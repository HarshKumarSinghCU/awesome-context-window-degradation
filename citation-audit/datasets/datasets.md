# Long-Context Synthesis Datasets

- **QASPER**: https://allenai.org/data/qasper (Scientific document QA across 1,585 papers)
- **SCROLLS**: https://www.scrolls-benchmark.com/ (Standardized benchmark for 10k–100k token synthesis)
- **RULER Evaluation Suite**: https://github.com/hsiehpinghan/RULER (Synthetic/real context stress tests)
- **GovReport**: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ccdv/govreport-summarization (High-density research reports)
